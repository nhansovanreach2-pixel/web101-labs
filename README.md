    <h3> Ingredients </h3>
    <ul>
        <li>2 cutps cooked white rice</li>
        <li>2 eggs</li>
        <li>1 cup mixed begetables</li>
        <li>3 cloves garlic, minced</li>
        <li>2 tablespoons soy sauce</li>
        <li>1 tablespoon vegetable oil</li>
        <li>Salt and pepper to taste</li>
        <li>2 spring onions, chopped</li>
    </ul>

    <!-- list <ol> -->
    <h3> Step 1</h3>
    <ol>
        <li>Heat the oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.</li>
        <li>Add garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.</li>
        <li>Add the mixed begetables and cook for 2mitutes.</li>
        <li>Puch vegetables to the side. Crack the eggs into the pan and scramble them.</li>
        <li>Add the cooded rice and mix everthing together.</li>
        <li>Pour soy sauce over the rice and stir well.</li>
        <li>Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with spring  onions.</li>
        <li>Serve hot and enjoy the meal.</li>
    </ol>
