About Us

The story behind Acme Co.

Our Story

Acme Co. began in 2015 as a small workshop with one simple idea: make things people will actually keep. Our founder grew tired of disposable peoducts and wanted to being back the quality of an earlier ara — when things were built the last and made by hands that cared.

Over a decade later, where we've grown into a team of designers, makers, and customer advocates, but the original idea hasn't changed. We still make every peoduct as if we were going to use it ourselves.

Our Mission

To craft every goods that earn their place in your life — through quality materials, honest design, and the kind of finish you can feel in your hands.

Our Values

  1. Craft — We design slowly and build carefully. Shortcuts cost more than they save.
  2. Honestly — We tell you what something is made of , where it came from, anda what it coasts us to make.
  3. Longevity — Peoducts should outlive thrends. Repairs should be possible. Disposable is not a value.
  4. People — Real customer, real makers, real conversations. We answer every email ourselves.
